Questions

Is this a physical book?

No. It’s an instant PDF you can read on your phone, tablet or computer, or print at home.

What stage of dementia is it for?

Any stage. It’s organized by problem, not timeline, and it’s most helpful for families in the early to middle stages who feel lost.

Is it specific to the US?

The legal and money sections are based on US programs like Medicare and Medicaid. Everything else, like communication, safety and caring for yourself, applies anywhere.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. It helps you understand your options, prepare for hard conversations and ask better questions. Always check with your parent’s doctors and a lawyer in your state before big decisions.
